03 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q1Is a flight permit required?
Yes. An application to the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism is required. Butterfly can handle the application for you.
Q2What happens in bad weather?
Flights are suspended in strong wind or rain for safety. We monitor conditions in real time with weather sensors.
Q3How long can the screen be up?
From a one-day event to a campaign over a set period. We plan it with you.
Q4What content can be shown?
Still images, video and live feeds. Please ask us about content production as well.
Q5How much does it cost?
It depends on scale, duration and venue. Tell us what you have in mind and we will take it from there.
