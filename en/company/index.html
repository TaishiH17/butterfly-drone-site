TeamMembers

PhilosophyOur philosophy

Excitement for everyone, by drone.

The sky is Japan's widest field, and one of the least used.

Through aerial displays, a new kind of media, Butterfly aims to make the city experience more fun and more beautiful.

The word “drone” originally means a male bee. Drones are often talked about alongside war and the military, and some people may feel a little guarded about them.

But drones can be so much more: freer, more joyful, and full of possibility. Our name, Butterfly, is the insect. Our small wings will set off the butterfly effect.
